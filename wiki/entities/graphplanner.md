# GraphPlanner: Graph Memory-Augmented Agentic Routing for Multi-Agent LLMs

## Summary
- Improves accuracy by up to 9.3% while reducing GPU cost from 186.26 GiB to 1.04 GiB
- Heterogeneous graph captures interaction memories among queries, agents, and responses
- Supports both inductive and transductive inference with historical memory leverage

## Relevance to Membrane
Relevant to Layer 2 (Shared Medium) and Layer 3 (Coordination). Uses a heterogeneous graph (GARNet) to capture interaction memories among queries, agents, and responses -- exactly the kind of shared substrate the membrane proposes.
