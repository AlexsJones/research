# Trust, Lies, and Long Memories: Emergent Social Dynamics and Reputation in Multi-Round Avalon with LLM Agents

## Summary
- 188 games of Avalon with LLM agents
- Reputation dynamics emerge organically with cross-game memory
- High-reputation players get 46% more team inclusions
- Higher reasoning effort enables strategic deception

## Relevance to Membrane
Critical for Layer 1 (Permeability -- Trust). Demonstrates that reputation dynamics emerge organically when agents retain cross-game memory. High-reputation players receive 46% more team inclusions. Supports our trust-based permeability model.
